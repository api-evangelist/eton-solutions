Where should a family office start with AI? The fastest, lowest-risk place to start is with high-volume, rules-based document work — not investment decisions. The five EtonAI™ workflows family offices deploy first are portal extraction, invoice and bill processing, statement reconciliation, capital-call handling, and portfolio analysis.
